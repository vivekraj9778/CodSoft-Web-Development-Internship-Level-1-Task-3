About the bakery

One starter, one oven, a hundred small decisions a day.

Wildgrain started in a home kitchen with a single jar of starter and a habit of baking before sunrise. Every loaf still begins the same way: a levain built the night before, folded by hand, and given time rather than heat to do the work.

We mill a portion of our own flour, keep batches small enough to shape by feel, and bake in a deck oven that was rebuilt brick by brick the year we opened our doors.

The signature: how a loaf actually happens

One bread, three days, four moments that matter.

DAY 1 · 8PM

Feed the levain

Starter is fed with flour and water and left overnight until it doubles and smells faintly of yogurt.

DAY 2 · 9AM

Mix & autolyse

Flour and water rest together before the levain and salt are folded in by hand, no mixer involved.

DAY 2 · 6PM

Shape & cold retard

Dough is shaped, set in bannetons, and moved to the walk-in for a slow, cold overnight rise.

DAY 3 · 6AM

Score & bake

Straight from the fridge into a 480°F deck oven with steam, scored by hand just before it goes in.
